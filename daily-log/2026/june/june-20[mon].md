# started work on the hammock

For the hammock I did'nt have proper thread and only had a really thin one
Me and a few others tested out the strenght of it, initally just by itself (it was much stronger than expected, but still not enough)
roughly 15 strands were needed to support a person.

Then for weaving a thicker string out of the thinner one I made a small disc with 6 poles (made out of straws, we also had way more than enough of these small thread so i just went with is)
but it turned out that i did not need this thing (as I was making it i didnt actually know how exactly it should work)
Then i just tied 6 threads together and started spinning them

but if you do do that you can't get a usable string as it would continously tend to curl and bend. (thought about making an actual weaving mechine)
then thought about using that itself to my advantage as in like let it curl up but in a controlled way.
Doing this i got a suprisingly well looking and stable thread, *It's hard to explain exactly how it looks in word, I might start posting photos of what all i do soon*
the thread no longr needed to be strongly twisted at both ends as after I do this it itself would hold.

for me to use for my "rotational torque" project, i could also use it for twisting the threads as it was quite time consuming
